School of Software Technology, Zhejiang University

Hi! I am a Distinguished Research Fellow at Zhejiang University. I received my Ph.D. from the State Key Laboratory of CAD&CG at Zhejiang University in 2022, advised by Prof. Xiaofei He and Prof. Deng Cai, and my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Zhejiang University in 2016. In 2019, I worked as a research assistant at the University of California, Los Angeles, with Prof. Quanquan Gu (Sloan Research Fellow). Since 2020, I have also worked with Dr. Wei Liu (IEEE Fellow) at Tencent AI Lab.

My current research focuses on Diffusion Models, Large Language Models, and AI Agents. I received the KDD 2026 Best Paper Award (ADS Honorable Mention) as the sole corresponding author. I am also the co-founder and CEO of DarwinMind.

Join My Group

My expectations are that you have a genuine interest in technology, are self-motivated, and can make full use of AI across all aspects of your work. I am neither a “push-style” supervisor nor a “sheep-style” (🐑, completely hands-off) supervisor. I believe the relationship between students and their supervisor should be a collaborative partnership built on a shared understanding. I can provide computing resources, help assess and refine research topics, and recommend students for internships at major technology companies and research exchanges at leading universities abroad. Students can pursue impactful research papers or build AI applications and products with genuine practical value. I have maintained good collaborative relationships with my past students. If you would like a more candid picture of me as a supervisor, you are welcome to contact my students.
If you are interested in joining my group, I generally expect you to rank in the top 5% of your undergraduate cohort at your university. This requirement may be relaxed for students from top universities, students with awards in programming competitions, or students who are among the first two authors of a paper published at a CCF-A venue. I also expect you to have completed at least one research project from start to finish. What matters is that you understand every detail of your research experience, rather than doing superficial work just to decorate your CV. I can readily assess whether you have actually implemented the research yourself. If you meet these requirements, you are welcome to send a one-page PDF CV to wuboxi@zju.edu.cn.
